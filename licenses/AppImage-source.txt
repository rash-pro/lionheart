It applies patches/libfuse/mount.c.diff and links the library statically into
the launcher. See BUILD.md and scripts/docker/build-with-docker.sh to rebuild.
You may modify libfuse and relink the runtime using those scripts. Replace the
runtime prefix with your rebuilt runtime and append the unchanged squashfs
payload (the original --appimage-offset gives its starting byte). Alternatively
extract with --appimage-extract and run squashfs-root/AppRun directly.
The launcher is a separate executable; the player does not link to libfuse.
